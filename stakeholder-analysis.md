1. Stakeholder analysis
The case describes a university library that relies on manual or outdated records.
The proposed LMS will organize book and copy records, membership,
borrowing, returns, reservations and overdue information. This report follows
the scope and work packages 2.1–2.3 of Assignment 1.
The needs below are inferred from the case scenario. No stakeholder interview
records were supplied, so these are proposed needs, not interview findings.
